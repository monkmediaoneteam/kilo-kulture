THE KILO KULTURE WAY

BRANDED CLOTHES. PRICED BYWEIGHT.

At Kilo Kulture, fashion meets a whole new way of shopping. Discover a curated collection of branded clothing, explore unique styles, and find pieces that match your personality. From everyday essentials to standout fashion, there's something for everyone.

Here's what makes us different: You don't pay according to the brand or label. Every piece is priced by its weight. Simply pick what you love, weigh your selection, and pay accordingly. More style, more choice, and a smarter way to shop.

Reading the scale

Light clothes stretch a kilo further. Heavy clothes use it up faster. Typical weights, so you can plan.

    Questions people ask at the counter.

    How is the price worked out?

    By the weight of what you pick, on the store scale. The rate per kilo is shown in store. Whatever the brand or style, the same rate applies.

    Are the clothes really branded?

    Yes. The stock is branded clothing. The labels you find change from rack to rack and week to week, so it is worth looking through.

    How do I get more pieces for my kilo?

    Choose lighter garments. T-shirts, shirts, kurtis and kidswear weigh far less than jeans, sweatshirts and jackets, so the same kilo holds more of them.

    How do I see what is new?

    Open the New Arrivals highlight on our Instagram. New stock is shown there first.
